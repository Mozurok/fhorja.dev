# ADR-0249: The 98 command descriptions are cut by 39.5 percent with every cross-reference kept, no case flipped on Sonnet, Opus or Codex, Haiku cannot confirm it, and the probe cannot show equivalence

- **Status**: Accepted. Its provisional decisions P-1, P-5, P-7, P-9, P-10, P-13, P-14 and P-15 were resolved by the maintainer on 2026-10-09 through locked decisions D-1 to D-6, kept in the maintainer's local task memory, as listed under `## Notes`. D-2, D-3 and D-4 carry `Impact: high`.
- **Date**: 2026-10-09
- **Tags**: advertise-stage, skill-descriptions, routing-probe, measured, guards, scenario-132, scenario-133, adr-0135, adr-0151, adr-0155

## Context

The Advertise stage is the `description` of every skill, loaded into every session before any skill body is read. ADR-0135 set an aggregate ceiling on it. ADR-0151 built the routing check that ADR-0135 said a trim needed. ADR-0154, ADR-0155 and ADR-0157 trimmed 30 flat descriptions and the persona commands, stopped on marginal value (ADR-0155 D-4; its D-3 projected about 6,356 characters left, and ADR-0157 revised that to about 5,000 for the 60 descriptions still untrimmed), and left the option open "on request". The maintainer asked for the rest.

At the start of this task the 98 descriptions held 72,808 characters, about 18,202 tokens at the repository's 4 characters per token, and the mean description ran 743 characters. A default run of `scripts/sync-workflow-slash-commands.sh` mirrors every skill, so the default advertises all 98. The profiles are opt-in filters (`--profile`): `minimal` (24 skills) carried 19,284 characters, `core` (52 skills) 41,077 and `full` (98) 72,808. The aggregate ceiling stood at 84,000 characters.

Two things differ from the earlier trims. The probe now has two case sets (`cases-98.json` and a terse paraphrase set, `cases-98-terse.json`) and is run on four model tiers with a shuffled control, in an environment where no Fhorja skill is visible to the model. And the baseline is no longer 100 percent: on the 98-case set it reads Haiku 86.4, Sonnet 98.0, Opus 99.0 and Codex 99.0, so a loss shows as a missed case rather than disappearing into a perfect score.

## Decision

Rewrite all 98 descriptions, longest first, in three batches, keeping everything the structural guards read and everything a reviewer could show a model needs, and measure the result on four tiers.

1. **Floor.** Every description keeps all of its cross-references (290 in `evals/skill-description-baseline.json`, which is not regenerated, P-10), its `Use when` and `Do not use` markers, at least 150 characters of capability text before the first marker, its worked examples, its named modes and flags, and its SHALL requirements.
2. **Method.** Mechanical Sonnet workers drafted about five descriptions each under a length cap (55 percent of the current length in batch 1, 60 percent in batch 3). The session validated every draft against the guards, read all 98 old and new texts, and restored cuts that lost meaning. Nothing was generated by a transform.
3. **Measurement.** A per-batch Sonnet check (one replicate) during the trim. Then the full probe on the trimmed tree: Haiku, Sonnet, Opus and Codex, conditions `A_full`, `D_shuffled` and `E_null`, three replicates, with case and command order identical to the baseline run. The terse set was run before and after on Haiku and Opus. A flip is a command the pre-trim text answered correctly in the majority of replicates and the trimmed text does not (P-1).
4. **Review pass.** Before the pull request, three read-only reviewers read the whole branch (descriptions, guard code, this ADR). The description reviewer read all 98 old and new pairs and found losses the probe could not show; they were restored (see Results). The author had read the same pairs and missed them.
5. **Stop rule.** The trim stops when two batches in a row save under 3 percent, when half is reached, or when a guard binds (P-14). The third happened: the capability floor and the references bind on the short descriptions, and half (36,404 characters) was not reached. All 98 were also done.
6. **Ratchet.** The aggregate ceiling in `check_advertise_stage_budget` moves from 21,000 tokens (84,000 characters) to 11,505 tokens (46,020 characters), exactly the final total plus 2,000 characters (P-7). `evals/scripts/guard-mutation.py` gains an entry that makes the aggregate exactly one character past the ceiling and requires the check to fail.
7. **No equivalence claim.** The probe near its ceiling can detect a loss of one or two cases on the strong tiers. It was not an equivalence test and this ADR does not claim one.

## Results

### Size

| | before | after | saved |
|---|---|---|---|
| all 98 (`full`, and what a default sync advertises) | 72,808 chars (18,202 tokens) | 44,020 chars (11,005 tokens) | 28,788 chars, 39.5 percent, about 7,197 tokens |
| `core` (52 skills, opt-in) | 41,077 | 24,359 | 16,718, 40.7 percent, about 4,179 tokens |
| `minimal` (24 skills, opt-in) | 19,284 | 11,650 | 7,634, 39.6 percent, about 1,908 tokens |

By batch: the 33 longest 29,610 to 16,340; the next 33, 25,140 to 14,373; the 32 shortest, 18,058 to 12,195. Later edits added 1,112 characters back: 37 to `feature-library-scout` in batch 3, 77 for the two probe-found fixes below, and 998 restored after the review pass. The mean description is now 449 characters. Tokens are characters divided by four, the repository's rule; billing of cached Advertise tokens was not measured, so these are not dollar savings.

|---|---|---|---|
| Haiku | 86.4 [98, 82, 80] | 92.9 [100, 82, 97] | not re-run |
| Sonnet | 98.0 [98, 98, 98] | 99.3 [98, 100, 100] | 98.0 (96 of 98) |
| Opus | 99.0 [99, 99, 99] | 99.0 [99, 99, 99] | 99.0 (97 of 98) |

The probed tree is the one after the two probe-found fixes and before the review-pass restorations. `routing-probe.py score` exited 0 on every tier there, and the shuffled control collapsed to 0.0 to 2.0 percent per run across the task (ceiling 5.1, n=98), so every tier reads the descriptions. Declined requests (`E_null`), on the tree before the two fixes only: Haiku 86.7 (baseline 73.3), Sonnet, Opus and Codex 90.0 (unchanged).

The final tree, with the review-pass restorations, got one `A_full` replicate on Sonnet, Opus and Codex and none on Haiku, with no control re-run. Compared case by case with replicate 1 of the baseline, no case differs on any of the three tiers. Under the majority rule against one replicate Sonnet shows `feature-library-scout` and its fleet swapped, which the baseline's replicate 1 also did.

Flips on the probed tree: 0 on Sonnet, Opus and Codex. Haiku also reads 0, but that reading is not safe. Its replicates differ in regime (next section): the probed tree had two long-reasoning replicates against one at baseline, and `code-context-map`, `resolve-contract-gaps` and `verify-against-rubric-fleet` pass the majority only because of that. Nobody read the probed tree 6 versus 6. The Haiku rise from 86.4 to 92.9 is the regime effect and is not a result.

Terse set, one replicate: Opus 99.0 before and 99.0 after, with no case changed, on the first trimmed tree and again on the tree after the two fixes. Haiku 87 correct of 98 before and 87 on the first trimmed tree; on the tree after the two fixes 81, then 86 on a repeat, against 84 for a repeat on the old text. The ranges overlap.

### Flips and weaker cases, and how each was resolved

- **task-init-fleet lost its worked example** (batch 2, Sonnet check). Restored.
- **feature-library-scout and its fleet** swapped on the Sonnet check in batch 3. A sentence naming the threshold ("more than 3 feature problems needing a deep sweep") moved to the scout side. The pair is still noise-unstable on Sonnet and Haiku and is named as a weak pair below.
- **task-init-fleet went from 3 of 3 to 2 of 3 on Sonnet and on Opus** in the full probe (replicate 3, answered `task-init`). That is weaker, not a flip, because the majority still holds. The trim had shortened `task-init`'s closing pointer from "For a brief with 3 or more independent sub-tasks" to "For 3 or more sub-tasks". The original sentence was restored. Re-probe: 3 of 3 on Sonnet, Opus and Codex.
- **code-context-map fell from 5 of 6 to 1 of 6 on Haiku**, every miss answered `code-locate`, the largest and most consistent downward swing in a 6 versus 6 comparison (the largest upward swing in the same comparison was also 4). `code-locate` now says "where one specific behavior lives" and excludes "a whole-repo overview or skeleton", with no cross-reference added. Re-probe: 2 of 3 on Haiku, 3 of 3 on the other tiers.
- **Seven apparent Haiku flips** on the three-replicate rule were mostly noise. Haiku answers in two regimes: with long reasoning (3,800 to 6,100 output tokens) it scores 97 to 100, and without reasoning (785 output tokens, the bare 98-line answer) 76 to 87, on the same text, at random. The long replicate moved from replicate 1 to replicate 3 between the runs. Three more replicates on each side gave 80.0 against 81.0 correct per replicate of 98, six cases down by two or more and six up by two or more. Three cases that fell by one or two replicates (`postmortem-author`, `resolve-contract-gaps`, `verify-against-rubric-fleet`) were left alone and are listed in the task's `flips.md`.

### Review pass

The description reviewer found 15 groups of losses that no guard or probe reported. The ones that changed when a command applies or hid a flag or mode: `incident-triage` ("urgency is real or unclear" had become "and urgency ... is unclear", which dropped an obvious production alert), `decision-interview` (the provisional, non-blocking behavior and the trigger outside planning), `direction-adjust` (its Use-when had become "when it is worth recording"), `db-context-supabase` (the CLI fallback), `pr-package` (no longer said PR), `implement-approved-slice`, `review-hard` and `security-review` (the `--tdd` and `--consistency N` modes), `impact-analysis` ("valid"), `frontend-architecture-review` (the coordination-pain condition), `team-update` and `skill-vet` (safety conditions), and broken grammar in four more. 29 edits across 25 descriptions restored or repaired them, adding 998 characters. Not done: naming `code-context-map` in `code-locate`, because a new cross-reference makes the baseline check fail and P-10 keeps that file unchanged. The restorations got replicate 1 on three tiers and nothing on Haiku.

### Side effect on the generated skills

`direction-adjust` (source file 20,160 to 19,944 bytes) and `frontend-system-design` (20,124 to 19,840) fell under the 20,000-character re-injection cap, which the generator measures on the source file, so it no longer adds the "Output contract, in brief" summary block to them (53 skills carried it, 51 do). They are no longer truncated after a compaction. `direction-adjust` sits 56 bytes under the cap.

### What was dropped

Long descriptive clauses and anything a model does not need to choose a command: file lists inside a description (`task-init` no longer lists the five files it creates), "Produces X.md" sentences repeated in the body, persona openers ("Senior reliability engineer..."), ADR numbers and `wos/` pointers, and repeated or doubled exclusions. The `docs/command-catalog.html` and `.json` pages show the shorter text too. Every old and new pair is in the task's `evidence/batch1`, `batch2` and `batch3` `pairs.md`.

## Consequences

### Positive

- 39.5 percent fewer Advertise characters (about 7,197 tokens) in a default sync, and about 1,908 tokens for a run that filters to `minimal`.
- Every cross-reference, routing marker and capability floor survives; the guards that read them pass (structural 91 of 91, lint, the description baseline check with the baseline file unchanged).
- No flip on Sonnet, Opus or Codex on the probed tree, no change on Opus on the terse set, and a review pass that found and restored losses lint, the structural suite and the probe could not see.
- The ceiling is 46,020 characters, exactly 2,000 above the measured total. A mutation entry proves the check fails above it (measured on a copy of the real skills: +2,000 characters pass, +2,001 fail).

### Negative

- Half was not reached (39.5 percent, 7,616 characters short). The references and the 150-character floor bind on the short descriptions, as ADR-0155 D-2 found. This change recovered 28,788 characters, against 5,000 projected for the 60 untrimmed descriptions in ADR-0157. The two figures do not measure the same population: this change also re-trimmed the 38 descriptions the earlier ADRs had already cut, and no per-population figure was computed. The rules differed too: this trim cut file lists, persona openers and doubled exclusions that the earlier batches kept.
- The ceiling now leaves 2,000 characters of room, about four descriptions at the new mean. A new command needs a trim elsewhere or a new decision.
- The mutation entry proves the check fires above whatever the constant is. It does not pin the constant: raising `ADVERTISE_BUDGET_TOKENS` leaves every mutation green. Only review of that diff line catches a loosening.
- Haiku neither confirms nor contradicts the other tiers: its regime noise is about 20 points and the final tree was not run on it.
- `code-locate` excludes "a whole-repo overview or skeleton" with no named route to `code-context-map`.
- The catalog page reads shorter for people too. Nothing here measured whether that is worse.

### Neutral

- Evidence limits. The probe can detect at most a one- or two-case loss on Sonnet, Opus and Codex (98 to 99 percent baseline), and cannot detect a one-case loss on Haiku, whose two regimes are about 20 points apart. The cases come from one author. The terse set was written by Sonnet workers and addresses that only in part. Every probe lists all 98 descriptions, while a real session loads a subset and history. No equivalence test was run on any comparison.
- Known weak pairs: `feature-library-scout` with its fleet, and `verify-against-rubric` with its fleet. Both were unstable before the trim.
- The `atom-audit` case in both case sets describes nine components, and the `atom-audit` and `atom-audit-fleet` descriptions split at six, so the case fits two commands. Opus and Codex were already wrong on it at baseline and still are. The case files were left unchanged so the before and after stay comparable.
- `E_null` was not re-run after the two fixes or the restorations.
- Probe runs: 118 recorded runs (none voided, no limit text) plus 2 isolation checks, 120 of the 120 allowed. Claude cost of the probe runs was US$ 10.73 (Haiku 0.23, Sonnet 3.57, Opus 6.94); Codex reports tokens only; the Sonnet drafting workers and the review agents are not counted. Installed skills are a confound the probe cannot see, so the harness runs with an empty working directory, `--setting-sources project,local`, no slash commands, no MCP servers and no tools, and for Codex a temporary home.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative 1: stay at 30 descriptions

- ADR-0155 D-4 stopped there on marginal value, when the ceiling was about twelve commands away (about fourteen after ADR-0157).
- Rejected: the maintainer asked for the rest, and the measured cut per description in this task was larger than the earlier projection.

### Alternative 2: the probe's mechanical `B_trim` transform

- Use the transform from the experiment as the edit.
- Rejected: ADR-0154 D-1 measured that it drops tracked cross-references. It stays an instrument.

### Alternative 3: drop references to reach half

- Cut cross-references on the short descriptions until the aggregate reaches 36,404.
- Rejected: ADR-0155 D-2 and the `description-reference-preservation` check of scenario 133 treat a dropped reference as the regression the gate exists to catch, and the pairs that confuse Haiku are the ones that point at each other.
